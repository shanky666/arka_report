11Data Sources and Methodology

Satellite Imagery: Copernicus Sentinel-2 Level-2A surface reflectance imagery at 10 metre spatial resolution from the European Space Agency. Cloud-masked median composites computed via Google Earth Engine. Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) formula: (Near-Infrared minus Red) divided by (Near-Infrared plus Red). Modified Soil-Adjusted Vegetation Index (MSAVI), Bare Soil Index (BSI), and Normalized Difference Water Index (NDWI) are additional spectral indices.

Climate and Rainfall: ERA5-Land monthly reanalysis from ECMWF and Copernicus at approximately 0.1 degree resolution, accessed via Google Earth Engine. Reference Evapotranspiration (ET0) estimated from ERA5-Land potential evaporation fields.

Soil Data: On-site SoiLENZ portable electrochemical soil test from Arkashine Innovations Pvt Ltd. Parameters extracted from the printed ticket via Optical Character Recognition using Tesseract.js version 5. All 14 parameters can be manually edited before generating the report.

Warning: This report combines satellite-derived reanalysis data with a single on-site portable soil test. Laboratory soil analysis, groundwater testing, and agronomist consultation are strongly recommended before implementing any remediation. Satellite values represent approximately 10-metre pixel estimates and may not capture exact micro-field conditions. This report is for preliminary diagnostic purposes only.
